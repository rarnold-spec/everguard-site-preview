Is it safe for my kids and pets?

Yes. Our products are 100% non-toxic and designed with safety in mind. We use eco-friendly ingredients that are non-toxic to humans, pets and the environment when applied according to the instructions. Our solutions are perfect for families who want to avoid harsh chemicals.

Will it wash off in the rain?

Our repellents are rain- and weather-resistant once dry. Deer and rabbit applications are designed to last about 4–5 weeks; tick and mosquito treatments last up to about 4 weeks. That’s why we come back on a schedule.

Why a seasonal program instead of a one-time spray?

Deer, rabbits, ticks and mosquitoes keep coming back all season. Scheduled visits keep protection in place as plants grow and pest pressure changes, so you’re never starting from scratch.

What if I still see pests after a treatment?

Call us. Under our Respray Guarantee, if pests return within 14 days of a treatment, we come back and reapply at no extra charge.

How much does it cost?

Programs start at $69. Your exact price depends on your property size, the type of pest and the frequency of treatments, and you save 5% when you prepay the season. Contact us for a free quote to receive an accurate estimate.

Where do you provide service?

All of Rhode Island, plus Mystic, North Stonington, Stonington, Pawcatuck and Groton in Connecticut, and North Dartmouth, South Dartmouth, Rehoboth, Seekonk and Westport in Massachusetts. Use the ZIP checker above or call us to confirm.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. Customers tell us we show up as scheduled and email ahead of time. Let us know about gates, pets or anything else when you request your quote.
